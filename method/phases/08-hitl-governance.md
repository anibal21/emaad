# Phase 08 — HITL Governance

## Purpose

Design optimal human approvals—minimal interrupts, maximum control where it matters.

## Do

1. Enumerate side effects from agents/MCP/scripts.  
2. Classify via [`../human-in-the-loop.md`](../human-in-the-loop.md).  
3. Assign approver roles and evidence requirements.  
4. Remove unnecessary gates; strengthen scopes instead.  
5. Ensure transparency: no hidden actions.  

## Exit criteria

- Gate **G-HITL** passed  
- Confirm-then-act list is short and high-signal  
- Autonomous paths still audited
